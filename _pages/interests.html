Research Interests

I was shocked that the AI disproved the conjecture of unit distance and the Jacobi conjecture in higher dimensions. I believe that the AI largely represents the future of mathematics. Considering the rise of AI, I hope my future research direction will be related to AI、machine learning or deep learning, etc. Additionally, I am very willing to study operations research and control theory. Optimization and control also have a strong appeal to me. Computational mathematics is also a good choice. This is because I really enjoyed the computational methods course this semester and I myself am also somewhat interested in algorithms.

Attitude Towards Research

I believe I have the passion and resilience needed for academic research. During the proof stage of my paper Locating a Shortest Vector in Certain 2-Dimensional Lattices, I experienced an unprecedented sense of focus and fulfillment. For that entire week, I thought about the details of the proof almost every waking moment. To verify the validity of my approach, I wrote programs to run extensive numerical simulations. One memorable moment came when I discovered an error in a key step of the proof — I pondered it repeatedly, even dreaming about it at night, and eventually found a successful fix. This experience taught me that genuine research demands sustained effort and perseverance, and I am willing to devote such energy to topics I care about without feeling fatigued.

PhD Intention

I'm not sure whether I will work or delve deeply into academic research. The latter requires a doctoral degree, and currently, I lean towards a master's degree, more oriented towards employment. However, I'm not opposed to the academic path either. This might be more based on my experience as a graduate student. I want to test whether I am suitable for the academic path before deciding whether to pursue a doctoral degree. I also hope to obtain a doctoral degree if this path suits me. The recent news that Wang Hong and Deng Yu won the Fields Medal is so inspiring. I can't help imagining myself becoming a pure mathematician and making some wonderful mathematical achievements. At this stage, I am more confused about the future. But I believe that with effort, the outcome won't be too bad.

Future Plans

I have two main paths for the future. One is to enter the industry directly after earning a master's degree — working at a major tech company always sounds very appealing. If I find genuine enjoyment during my master's studies, I would also be very willing to pursue a PhD.
